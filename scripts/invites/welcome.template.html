  1. Create your account

    Open {{SITE}}/signup and fill in four fields:

    • Display name — what the app calls you.
    • Invite code — paste {{CODE}}.
    • Email and Password (8 characters or more).

    No confirmation email to wait for. You're signed in straight away. If it says the code is invalid, check for a stray space or letter O vs zero; the code has no O, I, 0 or 1.

  2. Tell it about you

    Onboarding opens next. Every step is optional and has Skip for now, but the more you fill in, the better the coach can read your training.

    • The basics — height (cm), bodyweight (kg), birth year, sex.
    • How do you train? — days per week, experience (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced), preferred plan length (6–12 weeks).
    • What are you chasing? — tap goals in order of priority: Strength, Hypertrophy, Endurance, Mobility, Skill work.
    • Disciplines — e.g. Powerlifting, Hyrox, Calisthenics, Running.
    • Equipment — what you actually have access to.
    • Anything to work around? — sore or injured areas, plus a notes box.

    Each step saves as you go. Change any of it later in You → Settings → You.

  3. Pick a plan

    The last onboarding screen has Go to Plans →. Later, it's Training → Plan → Add plan. You get two tabs:

    Ready plan — fastestChoose from the Program list: {{READY_PLANS}}. Check the weeks and days per week in the preview, then tap Start …. It's copied into your account, so edit anything afterwards.
    Build my ownThree ways in:
    • Generate with an AI — download the context file, tap Copy prompt, paste both into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, then paste the answer back.
    • Upload a spreadsheet — download the CSV/TSV template, fill it in, upload.
    • Write it in Markdown — type the plan by hand.
    Check the preview, then Save & activate. If anything's off, a Compatibility list explains what, and Copy fix request gives your AI the corrections.

    Not sure? Start a ready plan. You can replace it any time, and your logged history stays.

  4. Set it up your way

    Open You → Settings. Worth a minute on day one:

    • Theme — Light, Dark or System.
    • Backdrop — Off, Notebook, Dotted, Hairlines, Topography or Depth.
    • Coach icon — pick a character (Gorilla, Hedgehog, Commando), colour or mono.
    • Body map — Plates, Engraved or Thermal.
    • Garmin — optional. Connect with your Garmin login, or import an export ZIP. Accounts with two-factor sign-in aren't supported yet.

    Appearance and body-map choices are saved on each device separately. Weights are in kg.

  5. Log your first workout

    Tap the round Log button in the middle of the bottom bar. Pick today's day from your plan and choose a length: Full, Express or Half. Enter weight, reps and how hard it felt (RPE), then finish.

    No plan day fits? Use Blank workout, or Log activity for a run, ride or class.

That's everything you need. Switch to Reference when you want to know what a screen or a word means.

Training → Plan Training tab

A review of how your plan is going, with View plan and Add plan. Inside View plan: a week strip, Edit (change phases, sections and movements; saves automatically) and New plan.

Training → Sessions Training tab

Reusable single workouts you've saved, plus days from your plans. Filter by workout type, duration and movements included or excluded. Good for "I have 30 minutes and a pull-up bar".

Training → Library Training tab

Every movement the app knows: its category, how it's measured, default rest, and which muscles it works. Shared library is everyone's; Yours is movements you added.

Progress → Stats Progress tab

Sessions, time and sets over your chosen period, by movement or by family (squat, hinge, press…).

Progress → Body Progress tab

A muscle map shaded by how much of your training each muscle got, with a Most worked list. Darker means a bigger share of your total, not "trained hard". Switch between Work and Volume, and between Strength, Cardio and Mobility.

Progress → Coach Progress tab

Tap Check-in to scan your recent sessions. Each finding says what it's based on and whether there's enough data to trust it. Act on it, snooze it, or mark it decided. Findings live under Open / Snoozed / Decided.

Review Training → Plan

The longer view: lifts per week, active days, hours, where your time went, hard sets per muscle per week, effort, and how your staple lifts moved from first to latest.

Words you'll see
Glossary
RPE
How hard a set felt, 5–10. 7 ≈ three more reps left in you, 8 ≈ the last good rep, 9 ≈ almost failure, 10 = nothing left. The logger starts at 7.
e1RM
Estimated one-rep max: the heaviest single you could probably lift, worked out from your sets. In kg.
Sections
A workout's parts: warm-up, primary, secondary, accessory, conditioning, cooldown.
Prep
Warm-up and cooldown. Logged and counted towards following your plan, but left out of volume, set counts, e1RM and RPE, so light warm-up sets don't drag your numbers down.
Phases
Stretches of a plan with one job: Accumulation (build volume), Intensification (heavier), Realization (peak), Deload (back off and recover).
Adherence
How much of your plan you actually did, shown as Plan X% · main Y%. Split into done as written, swapped for something similar, made up later, and missed. Extra work on one lift doesn't make up for skipping another.
Work
Body-map measure: each working set counts once, shared across the muscles it trains.
Volume
Body-map measure: load × reps × range of motion. Rewards heavy work more than Work does.
Period
The window Body and Stats look at: 4W, 6W, 8W (default), 12W or 6M. Set it once, both pages follow.
Check-in
The coach's scan of your recent training. Runs on your device; every finding names the rule behind it.
Express / Half
Shorter versions of a plan day for when time is tight.
